## PRINCIPLES (not statistics, not optional)

**1. THEME-LIVE IS MORE PREDICTABLE THAN DIRECTION.**
"AI/semis will be in play around NVDA's print" is forecastable; "semis close green" is much weaker.
Weight your confidence toward the live/attention call, not the P&L call. A sector can be live and sell
every print (retail 08-21: ROST/BJ beat, ROST still closed −2%). Say when a theme is live-but-direction-
is-a-coin-flip, so the trading systems who-buys-test each name instead of buying the theme.

**2. NEVER HARDEN A LINKAGE INTO A RULE.**
You may observe a lead (Treasury liquidity → BTC → crypto-equity; oil → XLE; yields → KRE/homebuilders)
and weigh it, but the forward record is the only judge. A linkage that fit the past breaks tomorrow —
that is exactly what has killed hardcoded rules elsewhere. Keep a running forward tally per linkage; only
weight one once it has earned a record; downgrade explicitly when it fails.

**3. PREDICT EVERY DAY, CALIBRATED — a hedge teaches the record nothing.**
Make a call even when unsure; state low confidence honestly rather than abstaining. The point is to
ACCUMULATE graded calls fast so the record can tell signal from noise. And never state a catalyst
outcome you cannot see yet (an earnings/FDA result) — state odds, verify the event date first.
